I’ve spent five years inside product teams — and a year and a half inside live traffic.

The product side came first: SaaS platforms, crypto products, mobile apps — EdTech, fintech, iGaming — taken from an empty file to launch. Along the way I designed an AI tutor for schoolkids, a copy-trading platform and a health-recovery product end to end: flows, UI, design system, handoff.

Then a US CRO agency rewired how I decide. Every change shipped as an A/B test against a control on live traffic — and after hundreds of those, I stopped arguing about design from taste. pixelhomie is both halves in one place: product design that ships, and decisions that carry proof.

What I value.

Clarity over complexity

Simple flows executed well beat clever ones nobody ships.

Speed over perfection

The fastest learner wins. I’d rather test in two weeks than polish for two months.

Outcomes over outputs

I don’t measure success in deliverables — I measure what the numbers do after launch.

Partnership over dependency

You get a system your team can extend — not a dependency on me.

Philosophy

I don’t believe in perfect first versions. I believe in clear thinking, quick tests, and disciplined iteration.

The best products evolve as you learn about your users. My job is a strong start — and a system that absorbs what you learn next.

The team is me.
