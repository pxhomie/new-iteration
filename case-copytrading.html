Trading that copies the pros for you.

The challenge

Everyone wants to invest. Almost nobody has time to become a trader.

Copy trading closes that gap: the platform mirrors the trades of top traders and strategies, in real time, straight into the user’s portfolio. The design job was to make a complex financial product feel simple — and serious enough to be trusted with money.

The branding

A dark canvas, a geometric logo — and one glowing line.

The identity is built on simplicity: geometric logo forms, a dark palette with soft blue gradients, and one signature element — a vertical glowing line, the scanner that copies trades in real time. Typography pairs a clean sans-serif for readability with an italic display face for character.

The interfaces

The website and the platform feel like one product.

The same dark theme, blue accents and typography run from the landing page into the dashboard, so the trust built on the first screen carries into the product. Key actions stay prominent, the data stays readable, and the motion stays smooth and quiet.

The results

Shipped in three months — brand, website and product UI as one system.

  • One identity across everything: the logo, the site
    and the platform speak the same language
  • The glowing-line scanner became the brand’s signature —
    from the logo to the product UI
  • A dark, data-heavy interface that stays readable at a glance
  • Handoff as a working system: typography, components
    and states developers could build from directly
