Designing ASI: an AI tutor that lives in a student’s pocket.

The challenge

Every student hits a wall at some point. Most can’t afford the private tutor who would get them past it.

Acton Street came with a clear brief: tutoring works, but it doesn’t scale. Students who need help at 11 p.m. get it two days later, teachers spend their evenings answering the same questions, and private tutors price out most families. The product had to put a personal AI tutor into every student’s pocket — one that answers around the clock, keeps learning engaging, and doesn’t feel like homework software.

The approach

Ten days, ten chat products, one conclusion: the tutor had to feel like a messenger the student already lives in.

I started with a teardown of ten chat apps to map what makes a conversation feel effortless. Two visual directions went to the client — strict monochrome and a bright, playful one — and monochrome won: black, white and gray with blue accents, set in Inter for clean multilingual text. From there the product took shape screen by screen: onboarding that configures your personal tutor, a home built around one tap to a lesson, the Talk to Your Tutor chat, achievements with mastery tracking, and multiplayer quizzes for competing with classmates. ASI also plugs into WhatsApp, Messenger and Discord — help lives where students already talk.

The Results

In two months the project went from brief to developer-ready: a complete mobile app with an AI tutor at its core, gamification that keeps students coming back, and a design system the dev team could ship from.

  • Full mobile app designed in 2 months — from research to developer handoff
  • 24/7 AI tutor: onboarding, chat, personalized feedback, mastery tracking
  • Gamified learning: achievements and multiplayer quizzes with classmates
  • 3 messaging platforms integrated: WhatsApp, Messenger and Discord
  • Monochrome design system with blue accents,
    set in Inter for multilingual UI
