FAQ

What exactly does Kora do? +

We are a growth partner for B2B companies in the $2M–$50M range. We diagnose what is holding revenue back, design the strategy to fix it, and then work inside your team until the fix is real. We do not run ad campaigns — we build the systems, processes, and positioning that make revenue predictable and repeatable.

What industries do you work with? +

Most of our work is in B2B — SaaS, professional services, fintech, and healthtech among others. What our clients share is a strong product that has outgrown its current growth engine. The frameworks travel across industries; the application is always tailored to your market, competitors, and buyers.

How is Kora different from a marketing agency? +

An agency executes tactics: ads, SEO, content. We operate a level up — figuring out why growth has stalled and which levers are actually worth pulling, then building the strategy and systems around them. If tactical execution is what you need afterwards, we will help you choose the right partner for it. In our experience the real gap is rarely tactics; it is clarity about priorities.

Who on our team would work with Kora? +

Usually the founder or CEO together with whoever owns the revenue number — a VP of Sales, a marketing lead, or a CRO. We embed in your existing rituals and tools and work directly with the people responsible for execution, so it feels less like hiring consultants and more like adding a senior teammate.

What happens on the discovery call? +

A focused 30-minute conversation about where growth is stalling and why. We ask about your funnel, team, and goals, share an honest first read on the bottleneck, and tell you whether — and how — we can help. No slides and no hard sell.

Is there a minimum commitment? +

The Growth Sprint runs eight weeks and is the smallest way to work with us. Partnership engagements are month-to-month after an initial quarter — if we stop earning our fee, you stop paying it.

Can I switch between tiers? +

Yes. Many clients start with a Sprint and roll into a Partnership once the roadmap is clear. You can step up, step down, or pause at the start of any month.

Do you work with startups or established companies? +

Both, with a sweet spot between $2M and $50M in revenue. Earlier than that, we help you build your first repeatable growth motion; later, we focus on removing the friction that scale has created.

What does the first week look like? +

Access, interviews, and data. We plug into your CRM and analytics, talk to the people who own revenue, and end the week with a working map of your funnel and the first hypotheses to test.

How do I know if Kora is the right fit? +

If you have a product that sells and growth that has stopped compounding, we are probably a fit. The discovery call exists to answer exactly this — and we will tell you plainly if we are not.

How long before we start seeing results? +

Quick wins usually land inside the first month — pricing fixes, funnel patches, cleaner reporting. Structural results build over one to two quarters as the new systems take hold.

How much time does this require from our team? +

Plan on a weekly working session with leadership plus async collaboration in your tools. We do the heavy lifting; your team stays close enough to own everything we build.

What tools or platforms do you work in? +

Yours. We work inside your existing CRM, analytics, and project stack rather than forcing a migration. Where a gap genuinely blocks growth, we recommend the lightest tool that closes it.

What does a typical engagement look like week to week? +

A standing strategy session, a shared scoreboard of the metrics that matter, and a prioritized backlog we execute together. Every week ends with what moved, what did not, and what comes next.

What kind of results do your clients typically see? +

Most engagements move the numbers that compound — faster pipeline, stronger close rates, lower acquisition costs — and several clients have doubled revenue within a year. On the discovery call we share case studies from companies at your stage so the benchmark is realistic.

Do you guarantee results? +

No honest consultancy can. What we do guarantee is full transparency: a scoreboard you can check at any time, and the option to end the engagement if the numbers stop justifying it.

How do you measure success? +

Against the revenue goals we set together in week one — pipeline created, conversion, velocity, and ultimately closed revenue. Activity metrics only count when they move those.

What happens after the engagement ends? +

You keep everything: the playbooks, the dashboards, and a team trained to run them. Most clients stay in touch with a quarterly check-in, but the goal is that you no longer need us.
