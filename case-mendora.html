A recovery platform that feels human.

The challenge

Recovery is hard work — the software for it shouldn’t feel like hospital equipment.

Mendora supports recovery after stroke and other neurological conditions: AI builds a personalized exercise plan, tracks progress in real time, and works both in the clinic and at home. The brief was direct — not another clinical tool. The platform had to feel warm, human and encouraging, and give a person clarity and calm on the way back.

The branding

Medical reliability on the outside, warmth on the inside.

The identity balances the two. A geometric display face gives the brand structure and clarity, while the palette — Blaze Orange with Power Blue — brings energy and positivity into a category that usually defaults to sterile white. Emotion-driven details make the product feel supportive, never clinical.

The interfaces

Designed for people whose focus and motor control are limited.

Light backgrounds with sharp contrast reduce visual strain, navigation is cut to the minimum, and every flow asks as little of the user as possible. Health data is presented simply, the typography stays clean with bold accents — the interface carries the person through the exercise instead of testing them with it.

The results

Shipped as one system — brand, product and site in four months.

  • An identity that reads medical-grade
    and human at the same time
  • The orange-and-blue palette became the product’s voice
    on every screen
  • Flows built for limited focus and motor control —
    nothing to figure out
  • Handoff as a working system: type, components
    and states ready to build from
